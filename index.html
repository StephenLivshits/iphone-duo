Zero-Crease Titanium Hinge

Formed with Grade 5 aerospace titanium micro-gears, the dual-action hinge guides the flexible display into a gentle teardrop radius. The result: completely zero visible or tactile crease when locked flat at 180 degrees.

Rated for 500,000 continuous folds 10+ Years Durability

Flexible Ceramic Shield

Engineered with an atomic-scale nanoceramic matrix infused into flexible ultra-thin glass. 4x more resilient to punctures and micro-abrasions than first-generation foldable polymer substrates.

0.03mm thickness profile ProMotion 1-120Hz

A20 Pro • TSMC 2nm

The world's first commercial 2-nanometer platform. Featuring a 6-core high-efficiency CPU, neural ray-tracing GPU, and split-motherboard thermal distribution allowing both phone chassis halves to act as unified heat sinks.

35% more power efficient 48 TOPS Neural Engine

Sapphire Power Button with Touch ID
