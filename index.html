Titanium Zero-Crease Micro-Gears

Engineering the vanishing line.

Grade 5 titanium synchro-gears draw the flexible display into an internal raindrop radius, removing surface fatigue.

Raindrop Cavity Hinge

Unlike traditional radius bends that pinch glass under acute tension, the Duo's titanium synchronized gears retract inward as the device closes, cradling the panel in a relaxed raindrop curvature.

500,000 Laboratory Cycles Tested

TSMC 2nm A20 Pro

Split across both titanium chassis halves, dual logic boards communicate across high-speed micro-ribbons through the hinge spine, providing twice the cooling surface area of any previous iPhone.

DuoOS Multitasking

Two apps. Zero friction.

Work across two continuous displays or spread a single app into wide-angle workspace.

Continuous App Handshake

Start a FaceTime call on the cover screen. Unfold the device, and the call automatically shifts to the left quadrant while your shared Keynote deck opens on the right.

Magnetic Pencil Pro Dock

Apple Pencil Pro snaps flush against the titanium spine, pairing instantly for pressure-sensitive drawing, photo retouching, and quick handwritten notes.

Adaptive Flex Stand Mode

Angle the hinge at 90 degrees on your desk. The bottom display instantly transforms into a full tactile keyboard and trackpad, while the top display presents your document.
